## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging contributions.
- Managing the issue tracker and project roadmap.
- Making decisions about project direction in accordance with the [governance model](GOVERNANCE.md).
- Upholding the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions and a clear understanding of the project's goals may be invited to become maintainers. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for details on roles and decision making.
